# Lattice Flow: Mathematical Glossary

A central lexicon for the cross-disciplinary terminology used in the Lattice Flow framework.

### Arithmetic Topology

The study of the analogies between number theory (specifically prime numbers and Galois groups) and 3D topology (knots and fundamental groups).

### Automorphic Form

A function on a group that is invariant under a specific discrete subgroup (like the Modular Group). In Lattice Flow, these are the stable "vibrations" or attractors that the simulation settles into.

### Betti Number ($B_n$)

A topological invariant representing the number of $n$-dimensional "holes" in a space.

- $B_0$: Number of connected components (Synchronization).
    
- $B_1$: Number of 1D loops (Circulation/Magnetic-like fields).
    
- $B_2$: Number of enclosed voids (Sinks/Sources).
    

### Bruhat-Tits Building

A geometric structure (often a tree or higher-dimensional simplicial complex) that acts as the discrete substrate for $p$-adic groups. It is the non-Euclidean "grid" of our simulation.

### Global Section

A set of node states (stalks) that are perfectly consistent across all restriction maps. In physics terms, this is a "Ground State" or a "Vacuum."

### Matter-Knot

A region of **Geometric Frustration** where the restriction maps prevent the existence of a local global section. This "knot" creates a persistent energy density visualized as a glowing singularity.

### Separatrix

A boundary in phase space that separates two different stable attractors. Crossing a separatrix often results in a **Topological Catastrophe** (a sudden jump in geometry).

### Sheaf Laplacian ($L_{\mathcal{F}}$)

A generalization of the graph Laplacian that incorporates the restriction maps of a sheaf. It is the primary operator used to compute the "Flow" and the "Spectral Embedding."

### Strange Correlator

A mathematical mapping that relates the topological order of the "Bulk" (the hidden lattice logic) to observable correlation functions on the "Boundary" (the PBR viewport).
